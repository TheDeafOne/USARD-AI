# Jefferson High School Visitor and Career Event Handbook

This fictional handbook establishes local procedures for visitors, career demonstrations, student privacy, room use, and technology at Jefferson High School. It applies to outside organizations invited to participate in school-sponsored career exploration activities. It does not authorize an event by itself; the school career coordinator remains the approving official.

## Career event hosting checklist

Jefferson career events use the Tuesday or Thursday window from 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Requests are due at least ten school days in advance, and the school must receive every adult visitor's full name 48 hours before arrival. Visitors check in through the main entrance with photo identification and remain with the assigned faculty host.

The standard event room holds no more than 30 students and provides movable tables, ordinary outlets, a whiteboard, and an HDMI projector. External visitors do not receive network access, so all essential content must work offline. Visitors may not collect student personal information, use lead forms, or photograph or record students without written authorization and the school's consent process.

## Scheduling windows

Career events may be scheduled on Tuesdays and Thursdays between 10:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. The approved window includes student movement into and out of the room, so presenters should design the active portion to fit comfortably inside it. Events may not be scheduled during testing blocks, emergency drills, or dates reserved for district professional development. The career coordinator confirms the final date and assigns the room.

Requests should be submitted at least ten school days before the preferred date. The request should identify the sponsoring organization, event topic, intended grade levels, equipment needs, and whether the activity includes a demonstration. A request is not final until the coordinator sends written confirmation.

## Visitor access and check-in

The school must receive the full names of all adult visitors no later than 48 hours before arrival. Substitutions made after that deadline require approval from the main office. Every visitor must enter through the main entrance, present government-issued photo identification, sign the visitor register, and wear the issued badge while on campus.

Visitors must remain with the assigned faculty host in student areas. Deliveries of demonstration materials should arrive with the presenting team unless the operations office has approved earlier delivery. Vehicles may use the east service entrance only when the confirmation email specifically authorizes it.

## Room capacity and layout

The standard career-event room has a maximum capacity of 30 students, excluding school staff and presenters. The room contains movable tables, a projector with an HDMI connection, a whiteboard, and ordinary 120-volt wall outlets. The center aisle and both exit paths must remain clear. Activities should be designed so students can observe without crowding around equipment.

The school can provide one accessible-height work surface when requested in advance. Presenters should provide a seated or non-contact alternative for any activity that requires standing, fine-motor work, or close proximity to demonstration equipment. The school does not provide specialized tools, protective equipment, extension cords, or adapters.

## Network and technology

External visitors do not receive access to the school network. Cellular coverage in the event room is unreliable. Any video, slide deck, interactive content, or reference material needed for the event must be available offline. Presenters should bring a local copy and a no-network alternative. Personal hotspots may not be connected to school-owned devices.

The room projector accepts HDMI input, but the school cannot guarantee compatibility with every adapter. Presenters should test their own laptop and adapter before arriving. Software installation on school devices is prohibited.

## Student privacy and information collection

Visitors may not collect student names, phone numbers, email addresses, social-media identifiers, photographs, academic records, or other personal data during the event. Interest cards, QR-code lead forms, sign-up sheets, and individual follow-up lists are prohibited. Students who request more information must be directed to the school career office, which distributes approved materials through school channels.

Photography, audio recording, and video recording are prohibited unless the principal has provided written authorization and the school has completed its own consent process. Presenters may record their equipment before students enter, but no student image, voice, name, or schoolwork may appear in the recording.

## Demonstration safety

All demonstrations must use training components or de-energized equipment. No fuel, compressed-gas cylinder, exposed blade, live electrical circuit, projectile, or controlled substance may be brought into the building. The faculty host may pause or end an activity when conditions differ from the approved plan.

Presenters must maintain control of demonstration materials. Students may handle only items that the approved activity identifies as safe for student use. A short safety orientation should precede any hands-on portion. School emergency procedures always take precedence over the event plan.

## Accessibility and student participation

Career events must be presented as exploratory learning opportunities. Participation in demonstrations is voluntary, and students may choose an observation role. Materials should use plain language, readable type, and captions or transcripts for essential video content. Requests for specific accommodations are coordinated through school staff rather than directly with individual students.

## Approval and day-of coordination

The career coordinator is the primary contact before the event. On the day of the event, the faculty host controls room access, student movement, and schedule changes. Presenters should arrive 30 minutes before the confirmed student start time for check-in and setup. Questions that are not resolved in the confirmation email should be treated as pending rather than assumed.
