# District Career Engagement and External Partner Policy

This fictional district policy governs career fairs, classroom speakers, demonstrations, workplace simulations, and other activities involving an external partner. School-specific handbooks may impose narrower requirements. When requirements differ, the stricter applicable rule controls until the school provides written clarification.

## Educational purpose

Every engagement must have a documented educational purpose connected to an approved course, career pathway, or school counseling objective. Activities should help students explore occupations, understand transferable skills, or connect classroom learning with broad workplace practices. An event may not be conditioned on a student providing personal information.

## Approval timeline

External partners should submit an event request at least ten school days in advance. The school identifies a faculty host, reviews the proposed content, confirms the space, and determines whether additional safety review is required. Equipment demonstrations, food, transportation, photography, and unusual power requirements must be disclosed with the request.

The district does not treat a calendar invitation, verbal conversation, or prior-year approval as current authorization. Written confirmation from the school is required for each event date.

## Privacy and contact information

External partners may provide approved public information, but they may not build a student contact list during a school event. Student names, personal email addresses, telephone numbers, photographs, demographic information, academic information, and individual expressions of interest are protected. School staff control any later distribution of approved materials.

## Neutral and accurate communication

Presenters must distinguish general career information from current eligibility, availability, compensation, or contractual terms. Claims about benefits, incentives, assignments, qualifications, and commitments must come from current authoritative material and must include applicable caveats. When a presenter cannot verify a detail, the appropriate response is to direct the question to the designated official resource.

## Safety and supervision

The school retains responsibility for student supervision and emergency procedures. The external partner retains responsibility for its equipment and materials. Students must have an observation alternative when an activity involves tools, protective equipment, physical contact, or close work around components.

## Accessibility

Partners should provide materials in an accessible format and coordinate accommodation needs through school staff. Activities may not single out a student based on disability, medical information, academic history, citizenship, family circumstances, or any other protected or sensitive characteristic.

## Records and after-action notes

The faculty host records the event date, topic, estimated attendance, participating classes, and any safety or policy issue. External partners may retain their own logistical notes, but those notes must not identify individual students. Lessons learned should focus on event design, scheduling, materials, and coordination.
