# Regional School Programs and Event Catalog

This fictional catalog helps planners distinguish among school programs and avoid applying one school's requirements to another. It contains useful distractor material for the workshop retrieval exercise.

## Roosevelt High health sciences

Roosevelt High emphasizes health sciences, patient communication, anatomy, public health, and clinical simulation. The school prefers healthcare career sessions and allows events on Wednesday afternoons. Its simulation room has a capacity of 24 students. These requirements apply to Roosevelt High, not Jefferson High.

## North County Tech

North County Tech offers advanced manufacturing, welding, electronics, and industrial maintenance. The school has a large lab and can support demonstrations involving approved training equipment. Events require seven school days of notice. Network access is available only through a preapproved guest account.

## Harbor High

Harbor High combines marine systems, logistics, and mechanical maintenance. Its program is interested in corrosion control, fluid systems, preventive maintenance, and supply operations. The school has no healthcare pathway in the current catalog.

## Oak Valley High

Oak Valley High offers transportation, mechanical systems, and general career exploration. It does not currently offer the district STEM presentation series. Presenters should coordinate through the school counseling office.

## Cyber event requirements

The district Cyber Careers Event normally uses a networked lab, approved practice accounts, and a two-hour instructional block. Network confirmation is required seven days before a cyber event. Those requirements do not describe the Mechanical Careers Demo and should not be imported into a mechanical event plan.

## Catalog limitations

This catalog summarizes program themes and common event formats. School handbooks and current confirmation messages control scheduling, visitor access, capacity, technology, privacy, and safety for a specific event.
