Story:
The class builds a Precision Recruiting Assistant that learns from historical recruiting outcomes to help a station decide where limited recruiter time is most likely to produce successful downstream outcomes, what type of engagement may be most effective at those schools, and what grounded information the recruiter needs to execute that engagement.Then the technologies have clean jobs:
Data pipeline: establish trustworthy history.
School ranking: identify where effort appears promising.
Recommender: identify what engagement may work there.
RAG: prepare the recruiter with grounded information.
Agent: coordinate the whole workflow.
Validation: determine when the system should and should not be trusted.
I think this is simple enough to teach as one story, sophisticated enough for a technical track, and much closer to the actual problems BG Dudley described than a generic “recommend content to high schools” exercise.
