Step 3 of 5 / Normalize

Move every vector onto the unit sphere.

Normalization removes magnitude and keeps direction. Points with a small angle between them have similar patterns—even if one school produces more contracts overall.

cos(θ) A · B‖A‖ × ‖B‖ = 1 when directions match

This sphere uses three visible features for intuition. The lab’s final score uses every action observed by both schools.

Step 4 of 5 / Measure the angle

Smaller angle, stronger neighbor.
